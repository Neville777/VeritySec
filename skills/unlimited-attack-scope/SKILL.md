## Unlimited Scope Principle

```
OODA / project blackboard Facts / verification rules / combo attacks apply to all security domains. A skill is a framework, not a cage:
  Search everything (multiple channels) · verify everything (search ≠ vulnerability) · chain everything (small flaws combine into a major breakthrough) · record everything (negative results are Facts too)
  No upper limit (Web/APK/EXE/internal network/cloud/firmware/blockchain/AI/wireless/hardware/social engineering all apply) · reroute when blocked · do not stop until the goal is reached
> Given a set of capabilities and a target, derive an attack chain unique to that target, the way solving a puzzle works —
> this chain may be applicable to only this one target in the entire world, and you will never find it by searching online, because you derived it on the spot.
> That is the real meaning of breaking out of the established framework.
```
