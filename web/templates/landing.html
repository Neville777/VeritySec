How it works

Three steps
to a pentest

01 — BYOK

Add your API key

Bring your own key — OpenAI, Anthropic, or any compatible provider. AES-256 encrypted at rest, never leaves the server. You control the AI, you own the costs.

02 — Chat

Open a conversation

Pick a security agent — pentester, forensics, cloud auditor, CTF solver — and start chatting. The AI asks before running anything dangerous.

03 — Hack

Paste your target

Drop a URL, IP, or domain. The AI runs recon, finds attack surfaces, exploits vulns, and generates evidence-backed findings with MITRE ATT&CK mappings.
